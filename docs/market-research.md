## Global signals (AppMagic H1 2026 casual report)

- Casual is about $11B in H1 2026, flat overall. Growth sits in **Merge-2 (+74%)**, **Life Sim (+76%)**,
  Sort puzzles (×3) and Farming (+15%).
- Coin looters (Coin Master, Monopoly GO) are about $1B per half-year, but **Coin Master was removed
  from Bazaar** by the criminal-content working group, so that genre is a regulatory risk in Iran.
- **BitLife** (text-based life sim, Candywriter, a tiny team) made about **USD 26M in 2019 at a 59%
  EBIT margin**. It was a top-5 most-downloaded iOS game in 2019, had 1.2M DAU and 7.8M MAU, and
  sold for $74M up front, rising to $195M with earn-outs. It still sits in the simulation
  top-grossing charts in 2026, with 50M+ Google Play installs and a 4.8★ rating from 1.8M reviews.
  It is the best proof that a *text* game with almost no art can be a big business.

## Iranian signals

- Bazaar has 80M+ yearly active users (1404 report). One user spent about 2.6 billion toman on a
  single game.
- About 29M active gamers, average age 29, 94.5% on mobile, more than half on budget phones.
  The Iranian share of spending fell from 40% to 6%, and the domestic charts are old and stagnant.
- Front-page games on Bazaar are estimated (unofficially) at 15–20 billion toman a month.
- **BitLife is popular in Iran but unusable as Iranian life.** It is listed on Myket and mirrored
  on many Persian APK sites (farsroid, 2android, gameeroid, coffeeapps…), all in English with
  American life (SAT, prom, dollars).
- **The only Persian life sim is «زندگی دیگران» (Mobidigo)**, with about 260k Bazaar installs (500k
  claimed overall) and v6.3 in Esfand 1404. It is a chat-with-characters story game (mafia,
  espionage, crypto), not a systemic life sim.
- Other gaps checked:
  - Monopoly GO-style dice builders: none in Persian, but too close to gambling for Bazaar.
  - Persian TriPeaks: none, but solitaire has weak cultural fit.
  - Screw puzzles: about 6 small clones (≤18k installs).
  - Online Hokm: fragmented (≤330k).
  - Persian farming: old offline titles (فارمولر 2015).

## Why a Persian life sim wins

1. **Proven model, tiny cost.** The content is writing and systems, which is exactly what we can
   produce at very high quality. There is no art bottleneck, and it runs on any budget phone,
   fully offline (shutdown-proof).
2. **A moat that translation can't copy.** The game is built from Iranian life itself:
   - the Konkur and its rank, the رشته choice at 15 and Sampad (تیزهوشان)
   - military service, its exemptions and buying it out
   - خواستگاری and مهریه in gold coins, عقد and عروسی
   - inflation, the dollar, gold coins and the housing market
   - emigration (IELTS, visas, the green-card lottery), a nose job, عیدی, Yalda with a Hafez fortune, and the aunt who asks "کی ازدواج می‌کنی؟"
3. **Viral by nature.** Every death produces an «اعلامیه‌ی ترحیم» share card (an Iranian obituary
   notice) summing up a ridiculous life. Iranian Instagram and Telegram meme culture is the
   cheapest acquisition channel there is.
4. **Broad audience.** Both genders, teens to 40s, the same people who pirate BitLife and play Amirza.
5. **Different from our other two games** (merge-2 for women 25–50, a hybrid-casual puzzle), so the
   portfolio spreads risk.
6. **Monetization that fits Iran.** Cheap one-time unlocks, which are what Iranians buy most, plus
   a 💎 currency for repeat purchases, plus Tapsell rewarded and interstitial ads.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Content regulation (ESRA / Bazaar review) | No politics, religion satire, alcohol, drugs-as-fun or sexual content. Romance is خواستگاری and marriage. "Crime" stays at bounced cheques, exam cheating and dodging service, always with consequences. |
| «زندگی دیگران» improves | Depth (systems, not scripts), humour, polish, the share card, generations. |
| Low ARPU of a text game | 💎 sinks (time machine, پارتی, instant cures), VIP, packs, rewarded ads. |
| Content exhaustion | Systemic events with conditions, plus random families, cities, traits and economy, give near-infinite variety. |
